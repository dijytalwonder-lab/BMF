Privacy Policy

Bunny's Magical Fishing · Last updated: August 28, 2026
This app collects no personal data

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Bunny's Magical Fishing ("the App", "we", "our") handles information. We built the App to be a simple, cozy offline game, and we designed it to respect your privacy: the App does not collect, store, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide any details such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves your game progress — such as coins, purchased decorations, day count, and best combo — locally on your own device only, using standard on-device storage. This information:

  • never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else;
  • is not accessible to us or to any third party;
  • is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Information Sharing

Because we do not collect any data, we have nothing to sell, share, or disclose. The App contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party software development kits that gather information.

Permissions

The App does not request access to sensitive device features such as your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files.

Children's Privacy

The App is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Internet Use

The App runs entirely on your device using content bundled inside it and does not require an internet connection to play. It does not send your information over the internet.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
